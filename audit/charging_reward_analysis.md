## Findings — Outputs of the Analysis

**Crossover temperature: 40.0°C.** Below 40.0°C, 160A maximises instantaneous reward. At and above 40.0°C, a lower current becomes reward-optimal.

**The crossover (40.0°C) is WITHIN the stress-test range [35–45°C] but OUTSIDE the normal training range [15–35°C].** The thermal derating incentive is only active during high-ambient stress-test episodes. This is the structural justification for the Experiment C stress-test distribution.
