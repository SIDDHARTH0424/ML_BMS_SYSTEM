                                  2. DETAILED PIPELINE ARCHITECTURE
====================================================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 TRACK A: FAST-CHARGING BMS PIPELINE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    +-----------------------+
    |   Environment State   |  SoC(t), V_term(t), T_batt(t), I_prev(t), T_amb, Safe_Fraction
    +-----------+-----------+
                |
                v
    +-----------------------+
    | PPO Policy / Baseline |  Observation Box(6,) --> Action in [-1.0, +1.0]
    +-----------+-----------+
                |
                | Remap: I_req = ((action + 1) / 2) * I_max
                v
    +-----------------------+
    |  Requested Current    |  I_requested in [0, 160 A]
    +-----------+-----------+
                |
                v
    +-----------------------+  Hard Safety Rules:
    | Unidirectional Safety |  • Thermal Derating: Linear taper 45°C -> 55°C (Cutoff >= 55°C)
    |     Layer (BMS)       |  • SoC Tapering: Smooth cosine taper approaching target SoC (95%)
    +-----------+-----------+  • Voltage Limit: Taper approaching V_max (403.2 V)
                |
                | I_applied = min(I_requested, I_safe_ceiling)
                v
    +-----------------------+
    |   Applied Current     |  Guaranteed safe charging current
    +-----------+-----------+
                |
                v
    +-----------------------+  • OCV Lookup: V_oc(SoC) polynomial
    |  1RC Thevenin Battery |  • Dynamic Polarization: dV_rc/dt = -V_rc/(R1*C1) + I_applied/C1
    |       ECM Model       |  • Terminal Voltage: V_terminal = V_oc - V_rc - I_applied*R0
    +-----------+-----------+  • Thermal Dynamics: C_th * dT/dt = Q_gen - (T_batt - T_amb)/R_th
                |                where Q_gen = I_applied^2 * R0 + (V_rc^2 / R1)
                v
    +-----------------------+
    |   Reward & Next State |  R = R_charge - lambda_T * (T - T_ref)_+^2 - lambda_smooth * (dI)^2
    +-----------------------+


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2 TRACK B: DRIVING ENERGY MANAGEMENT (EMS) PIPELINE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    +-----------------------+
    |  Standard Drive Cycle |  EPA UDDS / EPA HWFET / EPA US06 / WLTP Class 3b
    +-----------+-----------+  v_target(t), a_target(t), grade(t)
                |
                v
    +-----------------------+  F_drag = 0.5 * rho * Cd * A * v^2
    |  Vehicle Longitudinal |  F_roll = m * g * Crr * cos(theta)
    |       Dynamics        |  F_grade = m * g * sin(theta)
    +-----------+-----------+  F_accel = m * a
                |              F_total = F_drag + F_roll + F_grade + F_accel
                v              P_wheel = F_total * v
    +-----------------------+
    |   Wheel Power Demand  |  P_wheel > 0 (Traction) | P_wheel < 0 (Braking / Regen Opportunity)
    +-----------+-----------+
                |
                v
    +-----------------------+  Propulsion: P_dt = P_wheel / eta_propulsion
    |   Drivetrain Model    |  Regen:      P_dt = P_wheel * eta_regen
    +-----------+-----------+
                |
                v
    +-----------------------+  Observation Vector Box(11,):
    |  PPO EMS / Rule-Based |  [SoC, V_norm, T_norm, P_prev, v_norm, a_norm, grade, P_wheel, Regen_avail, T_amb, Progress]
    +-----------+-----------+  Output Action in [-1.0, +1.0] (Power distribution command)
                |
                v
    +-----------------------+
    | Desired Battery Power |  P_batt_req (Discharge if positive, Regen charge if negative)
    +-----------+-----------+
                |
                v
    +-----------------------+  Bidirectional Hard Constraints:
    |  Bidirectional Safety |  • Discharge Cap: Max discharge current limit
    |     Layer (EMS)       |  • Low SoC Derate: Prevents deep over-discharge below minimum SoC
    +-----------+-----------+  • Voltage Protections: Clamps at V_min (270V) and V_max (403.2V)
                |              • Thermal Protection: Derates discharge and charge under high temperatures
                v
    +-----------------------+
    | Actual Applied Power  |  Feasible P_applied (Battery power actually delivered/absorbed)
    +-----------+-----------+  Power Deficit = max(0, P_demand - P_applied)
                |
                v
    +-----------------------+  • Battery ECM steps with I_equiv = P_applied / V_terminal
    | Battery Dynamics Step |  • Updates SoC, V_rc, Heat, and Cell Temperature
    +-----------+-----------+
                |
                v
    +-----------------------+
    |     EMS Reward        |  R = Regen_Recovery_Bonus - Net_Energy_Cost - lambda_def * Power_Deficit
    +-----------------------+

====================================================================================================
                              4. SCIENTIFIC & EXPERIMENTAL FINDINGS
====================================================================================================

1. Validated Candidate vs Long-Horizon Instability:
   • Track A (Charging): Validated at A1 50k steps across 3 random seeds (7, 21, 42).
     Achieved mean charge time of 2101.5 s (Gate: <= 2199.3 s), 15/15 target-reached.
     Training for 1M steps resulted in policy collapse (documented as a research finding
     on PPO stability in deep RL).
   • Track B (Driving): Validated at B3 100k steps across 3 random seeds (7, 21, 42).
     Achieved cross-cycle mean 128.5 - 129.3 Wh/km with 97.4% - 99.9% regen recovery
     and exactly ZERO safety interventions across all 4 standard drive cycles.

2. Safety-First Architecture:
   • Hard safety constraints cannot be bypassed by RL policies.
   • Thermal derating and SoC/voltage limits guarantee operation within physical cell envelope.

3. Testing & Verification:
   • 225 / 225 Automated pytest tests passing.
   • compileall passes with zero syntax or import errors.
