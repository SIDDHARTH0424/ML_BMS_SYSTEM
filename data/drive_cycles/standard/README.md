# Standard Drive Cycles Repository

This directory contains verified, public-domain and open international standard drive cycles for EV energy management simulation:

| Cycle ID | Category | Name | Duration | Publisher | License |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `epa_udds` | Urban | EPA UDDS (Urban Dynamometer Driving Schedule) | 1372s | United States Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) | Public Domain (US Government Work, 17 U.S.C. § 105) |
| `epa_hwfet` | Highway | EPA HWFET (Highway Fuel Economy Test) | 765s | United States Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) | Public Domain (US Government Work, 17 U.S.C. § 105) |
| `epa_us06` | Aggressive | EPA US06 (Supplemental FTP - Aggressive Driving) | 596s | United States Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) | Public Domain (US Government Work, 17 U.S.C. § 105) |
| `wltp_class3b` | Mixed | WLTP Class 3b (Worldwide Harmonized Light Vehicles Test Procedure) | 1800s | United Nations Economic Commission for Europe (UNECE) | Open International Regulatory Standard |

All cycles are formatted with `time_s` and `speed_mps` at 1.0 Hz matching the simulation timestep.
